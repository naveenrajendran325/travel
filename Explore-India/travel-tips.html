Smart Travel Tips for India

A little preparation helps you travel comfortably, stay flexible and make thoughtful choices along the way.

1. Plan Your Journey

India is vast, so avoid packing too many far-apart places into a short trip. Decide what interests you most, allow time for travel between stops, and keep copies of important bookings and identification.

  1. Choose a few regions that fit your available time.
  2. Check transport schedules and book popular routes ahead.
  3. Share your itinerary and check-in details with someone you trust.

2. Choose the Right Season

Weather varies greatly by region. Many destinations are most comfortable in cooler months, while the monsoon brings heavy rain to some areas. Research the climate for each stop and check for local weather advisories before travelling.

3. Book Accommodation

Compare location, recent reviews, accessibility and cancellation policies—not only price. Confirm your booking directly with the property and save its address offline so you can find it when you arrive.

4. Pack Your Travel Essentials

Pack light and choose clothing suited to the weather and the places you plan to visit. A small day bag keeps essentials close at hand.

Packing checklist

  • Reusable water bottle and any personal medicines.
  • Comfortable walking shoes, sun protection and a light layer.
  • Chargers, power adapter and a portable battery.
  • Copies of travel documents and emergency contacts.
  • Hand sanitiser, tissues and a small first-aid kit.

5. Stay Safe During Travel

Use registered transport when possible, keep valuables secure, and trust your judgement in unfamiliar situations. Drink safe water, take breaks in hot weather and seek help from trusted local staff if you need assistance.

  • Save local emergency numbers and your accommodation contact.
  • Keep a small amount of cash separate from your main wallet.
  • Check official travel and health guidance before departure.

6. Respect Local Culture

Traditions differ between communities and places of worship. Dress modestly where appropriate, ask before photographing people, and follow posted guidance about shoes, conduct and restricted areas.

7. Save Money While Travelling

Plan a daily budget that includes meals, local transport and entrance fees. Compare fares before booking, eat at well-reviewed local spots, and use public transport where it is safe and convenient.

8. Responsible Tourism

Support local communities and help protect natural and cultural heritage. Carry reusable items, reduce waste, respect wildlife, and choose ethical activities that do not exploit people or animals.

Before you leave

Check your bookings, weather, travel documents and current local advice. Keep plans flexible—and treat people, places and the environment with care.
